WebCodecs Limited Support

Your browser does not natively support H.264 high-speed hardware encoding. MP4 exports will proceed via **MediaRecorder WebM format** which is standardly supported, and then downloaded with a .mp4/webm name extension, but may require VLC or a desktop encoder for full compatibility. For the best pixel-perfect MP4 experience, we recommend using **Google Chrome** or **Microsoft Edge**.
